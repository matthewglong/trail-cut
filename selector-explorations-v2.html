Round six. The Ink Stamp call was right — solid chartreuse fill is the root motif. The corner notches were gimmicky and the cartographic vocabulary was tapped. These six push the fill into hardware/TUI territory: Norton Commander selection bars, mixer-channel tally LEDs, outboard-gear transports, DOS terminal brackets, LCD readouts, mechanical keycaps. One accent — chartreuse. One reusable component. Click any option to feel the indicator move.

Transport

Each option is a physical button on an outboard-gear faceplate. The same idiom as a Reason rack or a Logic transport row. Tallest variant at 32 px — the picker has weight in the hand.
