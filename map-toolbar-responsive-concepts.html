/map-toolbar·responsive-studies eight strategies for a draggable-pane toolbar

The map toolbar lives on a resizable pane and currently clips its tail when the pane is narrowed. Each concept below preserves every control — scope, route mode, waypoint mode, follow, bearing, zoom, map style, positioning entry — and degrades differently as the pane shrinks.

⇿ drag the bottom-right corner of any demo to feel its compression behaviour. Container queries do the work — no JS reflow.

02

PRIORITY + OVERFLOW the industry default

priority tiersSlack · Office · Figma

Each tool declares a priority. As width shrinks, the lowest-priority tools collapse into a ••• menu on the right. Honest — users see an overflow marker — but the overflow menu is where features go to die. Best when most tools are set-once defaults that nobody returns to.

03

PROGRESSIVE COMPRESSION multi-stage degradation

container queriesFigma · Linear panels

Every tool defines its own compression stages. At wide widths everything reads in full. As the pane narrows the toolbar peels off the cheapest visual layer first: labels go before icons; stepper chrome before values; badges before glyphs. Nothing ever vanishes — state is always legible.

04

CONSOLIDATED CAMERA PILL group what is conceptually one thing

click to expandLinear filters · macOS menu extras

The right half of the toolbar (follow, bearing, zoom) is really one concept — camera behaviour. Collapse it into a single readable pill. Click the pill to expand the full strip in place. Reads as one fact at a glance, edits as a full strip when you need it.

05

HOVER-EXPAND CLUSTERS three category chips, inline expansion

hover or clickPhotoshop options bar · Fusion 360

Three category chips — DISPLAY, CAMERA, STYLE — each with a tiny status pip. Hover (or click) a chip and it expands inline, pushing siblings aside, revealing the full controls. Predictable hit targets and a very calm resting state.

06

COMMAND PALETTE two pinned tools, ⌘K for everything else

⌘KLinear · Raycast · Arc

Keep only the controls you reach for per-scrub on the bar (scope + follow). Push everything else to a command palette. Most map settings are set-once-per-session, not per-clip — they don't deserve permanent pixels. Click the input or hit ⌘K to bring up the palette below.

07

DISSOLVE INTO MAP tools relocate onto the surface they affect

surface anchorsApple Maps · Google Earth · Gaia GPS

The toolbar shrinks to a single status line; the tools relocate as semi-transparent chips anchored to the map's corners. Bearing & follow live near the compass, zoom sits with the gesture target, style toggle parks where base-layer controls always sit. Toolbar height halves — the map gets vertical pixels back.

08

RECOMMENDED HYBRID compress left · consolidate right

ship candidate03 + 04

The two halves of the toolbar want different treatments. The left half is discrete display toggles — they benefit from per-control compression (#03). The right half is one conceptual group — it benefits from consolidation (#04). Combining the two keeps every state visible at every width, with no overflow menu graveyard.
