SELECTOR
EXPLORATIONS

Six candidate treatments for the in-panel selector — the reusable component that will replace ModePicker inside DecorationPanel popovers, and is a candidate to absorb WaypointShapes as well. Each variant is shown at four densities so its behaviour at 2-, 3-, and 4-option strips and at 6-option icon grids can be compared in one read. Click any option to feel the indicator motion. Chartreuse is the only accent in play; everything else is wire, granite, and canvas.
